## AmOn Another Level (1-2) vs Los Angeles Akers (2-1)
### Final Score: AmOn Another Level (105.88) vs Los Angeles Akers (125.86)
Historically, these teams have some of the closest and entertaining matchups, and this one didn’t disappoint. On Sunday, they were neck and neck with exciting and controversial plays from both teams. Going into Monday, both teams’ projected score was at 110. But it seemed the Akers were on a mission as all went right for them during the MNF games. The LA Akers looked for vengeance and vengeance they got by beating one of their league rivals 125-105. 
> **_NOTE:_**  Team owner TJ Nanugonda changed his team name from Meet the Stevensons to AmOn Another Level

**Next Up:**
LA Akers 2-1 vs D&T 3-0 | AmOn 1-2 vs CNG 1-2

## Team ETERNAL PAIN (2-1) vs Certified Boys Lover (1-2)
### Final Score: Team ETERNAL PAIN (137.8) vs Certified Boys Lover (111.6)
After a heartbreaking loss in Week 2, Team EP’s manager Amogh seemed confident his players would bounce back. Facing a good CBL team, Team EP’s star WR Keenan Allen put up a monster 45 point performance. This and their Kicker/DST carried them past the pedo (I think he’s into young-men now), moving them to 2-1.

**Next Up:**
EP 2-1 vs Drako 0-3 | CBL 1-2 vs GWC 1-2

## Great Walker China (1-2) vs Chubbin’ N Grubbin (1-2)
### Final Score: Great Walker China (146.06) vs Chubbin’ N Grubbin (150.68)
Coming into this matchup CNG was 0-2 and without their star RB Nick Chubb for the year. It wasn’t looking good for them but they knew their roster can compete. Facing a strong GWC team, CNG put up a league’s season high 150 points on the backs of Raheem Mostert ‘s 45 points and all their skill positions scoring above 15 points. GWC came close by dropping the second highest point performance for this league’s season with 146, but they’ll have to move to 1-2 unluckily. 

**Next Up:**
CNG 1-2 vs AmOn 1-2 | GWC 1-2 vs CBL 1-2

## Team Drako (0-3) vs Cookin’ It Up (3-0)
### Final Score: Team Drako (99.06) vs Cookin’ It Up (113.0)
Coming into this matchup, Team Drako was last place and CIT was first place. Expecting a cake walk for CIT, both teams looked average at best with Team Drako projected to win going into MNF. Team Drako could not take advantage as their mediocrity continued with bad performances from Burrow and White. CIT continues his controversial run of wins and Team Drako is now the only team in the league that has not won a game. 

**Next Up:**
Drako 0-3 vs EP 2-1 | CIT 3-0 vs CM 2-1

## Code Monkey (2-1) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (1-2)
### Final Score: Code Monkey (141.98) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (118.02)
Coming off a tough week, Code Monkey faced a real test against the top scorer of week 2, the Baddies. Both teams came out firing in the early window but Code Monkey was able to find a slight lead. In the late window of games Code Monkey improved their lead and came out with a huge win. They’ll miss Mike Williams for the season but they’ll be expecting a return from their star RBs Jones and Ekeler. 

**Next Up:**
CM 2-1 vs CIT 3-0 | DPB 1-2 vs OG 1-2

## Olave Garden (1-2) vs Drake and Travis (3-0)
### Final Score: Olave Garden (111.16) vs Drake and Travis (139.48)
Two former roommates always had their fair share of battles but this matchup got ugly quick. D&T dominated the Sunday’s late window of games as OG failed to keep up. The three Eagles capped off a great week on MNF improving D&T to 3-0. Back to back weeks of struggle for Olave Garden as they hope key pieces will come back sooner than later.

**Next Up:**
D&T 3-0 vs LA Akers 2-1 | OG 1-2 vs DPB 1-2
