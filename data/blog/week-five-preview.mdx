## TNF (Prime Video)
### Code Monkey (2-2) vs Team Drako (0-4)
#### Line: Drako -10.8
Perfect for TNF, two poverty stricken teams in recent history face off in week 5. Team Drako has its sights on wins as their first round pick Cooper Kupp will likely return to action this week. Code Monkey has two starters out this week with no remarkable players left in the lineup outside of Mahomes.

##1:00 (CBS)
### Drake and Travis (4-0) vs Chubbin N’ Grubbin (1-3)
#### Line: D&T -17.9
Chubbin N’ Grubbin put up a 61 point dud last week (21 points from Jags D) and faces a Drake and Travis team that has been firing on all cylinders. Since Chubb’s injury no player has stepped up enough to warrant a name change. Kirk hopes that changes this week. If Drake and Travis keeps scoring TDs at the rate they’ve been, it won’t matter how good the other team performs.

## 1:00 (FOX)
### Team ETERNAL PAIN (3-1) vs Great Waller China (1-3)
#### Line: 
Team ETERNAL PAIN has started off strong this season and added a PPR cheat code Alvin Kamara along the way. They hope to continue their 2 game win streak against a somewhat unlucky Great Waller of China team that has the most PA.

## 4:25 (FOX)
### AmOn Another Level (2-2) vs Certified Boys Lover (2-2)
#### Line: GWC -4.1
The Certified Boys Lover should be pumped up this week with Drizzy dropping entering the weekend. They hope to take advantage of a middling AmOn Another Level team with a lot of injury designations entering Thursday night.

## SNF (NBC)
### Olave Garden (2-2) vs Cookin’ It Up (4-0)
#### Line: OG -10.4
We may have a shootout on our hands as these two teams both dropped over 140 last week. Olave Garden needs some of their highly drafted players to live up to their expectations to pull this one out. Cookin’ It Up looks to survive another week at the top of the table during a week without QB1.

## MNF (ESPN)
### Los Angeles Akers (2-2) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (1-3)
#### Line: Akers -10.2
Baddies manager Sam Nangali comes in peace this year, with a nonchalant management style. Team Akers wants all the smoke. The Akers hope to bounce back after a disappointing loss to stay above .500. This is also a crucial game for the Baddies as it may be tough to recover from a 1-4 start.
