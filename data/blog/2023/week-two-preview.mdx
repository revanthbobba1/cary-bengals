## TNF (Prime Video)
### Chubbin N’Grubbin (0-1) vs Youngboys Lover (0-1)
#### Line: CNG -1.5
Two teams coming off a loss week 1 look to regroup and bounce back. Manager Kirk Broadbelt will want to find his inner Chris Hansen as he’ll look to beat his former roommate and go out with a win week 2. 

## 1:00 (CBS)
### Cookin’ It Up (1-0) vs Team ETERNAL PAIN (1-0)
#### Line: CIU -9.3
Ever since Cookin’ It Up started his reign, Team EP hasn’t found much luck as they’ve been a bottom dweller since. Team EP wants to make a statement against the defending champs and move to 2-0. On the other hand, Cookin’ It Up wants to prove last week wasn’t a fluke and tell the league their team is for real. 

## 1:00 (Fox)
### Los Angeles Akers (1-0) vs Great Waller China (0-1)
#### Line: GWC -7.6
In the last six matchups, GWC is 5-1 against the LA Akers. They’ll hope to find their first win of the season this week. The Akers looked very good week 1. The commish looks to build on that great performance and find a way to beat an opponent they’ve had no luck beating in the past. 

## 4:25 (CBS)
### Drake and Travis (1-0) vs Code Monkey (1-0)
#### Line: D&T -15.4
Back in 2021, both team managers were going back and forth hoping they would meet each other in the championship. Code Monkey got left behind that year but they know the past is behind them as they’ll look to build on a strong performance from last week.

## SNF (NBC)
### Disney Pixar Baddies (0-1) vs Team Drako (0-1)
#### Line: DPB -11.5
After one of the worst performances I’ve ever seen from any team in this league, Team Drako will want to pull an 180 and find their first win. After an unlucky loss week 1, the Baddies will pregame hard as they prepare for primetime. 

## MNF (ESPN)
### Olave Garden (1-0) vs Meet the Stevensons (0-1)
#### Line: MTS -5.4
Meet the Stevensons manager TJ had much to say in week 1’s post game press conference. TJ offered comments like, “I got soft a** competition in this league” and “This league sorry as h*ll”. After an underwhelming performance week 1, Meet the Stevensons put themselves as the villain of the league. They face the best performer of week 1, Olave Garden, as both teams have a lot to prove moving forward.
