## TNF Opening night (NBC/ Peacock)
### Cookin’ It Up (Sparsh Patel) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (Sam Nangali)
#### Line: Baddies -2.6

The two time defending champions will look to start the season off with a positive note as they take on the Disney Pixar Baddies. The Baddies want to make a statement opening weekend and show they’re not to be fooled around with.

## 1:00 (FOX)
### Team ETERNAL PAIN (Amogh Natu) vs Chubbin N’ Grubbin (Kirk Broadbelt)
#### Line: PAIN -3.9

These two teams have been on the opposite ends of the table these last couple years but both owners continue to have high expectations going into the new year. 

## 1:00 (CBS)
### Team Drako (Carter Bilbro) vs Drake and Travis (Keshav Sridhar)
#### Line: Travis -5.2

A matchup with similar names and similar situations as both teams might be missing their first round pick week 1. D&T will be favored going into this matchup but Team Drako will want to prove otherwise.

## 4:25 (Fox) 
### Great Waller China (Alvin Bao) vs Olave Garden (Revanth Bobba)
#### Line: China -20.7

A rematch from the 2020 super bowl consists of two teams that seem to be around the same area of the table at the end of each season. Olave Garden will look to find an answer to his rb troubles for the opening weeks as GWC will want to put their name back to the top. 

## SNF America’s Game of the Week (NBC)
### Code Monkey (Ankith Kodali) vs Meet the Stevensons (TJ Nanugonda)
#### Line: Monkey -1.3

The biggest rivalry in the league will once again headline opening week. Code Monkey won this matchup comfortably last season but the celebrations soon came to an end as their season quickly went downhill. After losing this matchup, The Stevensons became active on the trade block resulting in their most successful season. These two have been going at it since birth and they simply don’t like each other.

## MNF (ESPN)
### YoungBoys Lover vs Los Angeles Akers
#### Line: Akers -2.4

The LA Akers look to build on their success from last year and use their momentum to win their opening week matchup. The Catholic Priests from Boston want to spoil commish’s opening week as they look to take advantage of their opponent. 




> **_NOTE:_**  Matchup of the week and the headliner matchup will have a post game press conference
