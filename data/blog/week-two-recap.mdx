## Chubbin’ N Grubbin (0-2) vs YoungBoys Lover (1-1)
### Final Score: Chubbin’ N Grubbin (102.36) vs YoungBoys Lover (129.24)
A great Thursday night gave YBL a slight lead going into Monday night. CNG could not catch the pedo due to a Nick Chubb season ending injury. YBL’s three receivers combined for 70.7 points leading their team to a victory.

## Cookin’ It Up (2-0) vs Team ETERNAL PAIN (1-1)
### Final Score: Cookin’ It Up (121.2) vs Team ETERNAL PAIN (120.94)
An eventful Sunday for both teams led to a  MNF player vs player matchup to decide the week’s result. After a Jamaal Williams’ hamstring injury, it seemed all hope was lost for CIT. But Najee Harris fell .3 points short for Team EP leading to a CIT win.

## Los Angeles Akers (1-1) vs Great Waller China (1-1)
### Final Score: Los Angeles Akers (124.3) vs Great Waller China (128.14)
After losing Week 1, the 3x champ knew his team needed to step up this week. After an eventful Sunday where the win/loss percentages kept changing, the LA Akers were the more likely to win this matchup going into MNF. Unfortunately the Akers came up short due to a mediocre performance from Deshaun Watson. GWC is now 6-1 against the LA Akers in the last 7 times they’ve met. 

## Drake and Travis (2-0) vs Code Monkey (1-1)
### Final Score: Drake and Travis (128.12) vs Code Monkey (101.4)
Both teams coming off a win week 1 looked to improve their record to 2-0. D&T’s great TNF was too much for the injury-riddled Code Monkey team. D&T improves to 2-0 and is now second place.

## Disney Pixar Baddies (1-1) vs Team Drako (0-2)
### Final Score: Disney Pixar Baddies (129.86) vs Team Drako (121.48)
Both teams looking for their first win in this primetime matchup. DPB’s WR performances was too much for Team Drako as the Baddies improve to 1-1. 

## Olave Garden (1-1) vs Meet the Stevensons (1-1)
### Final Score: Olave Garden (77.34) vs Meet the Stevensons (121.38)
MTS manager TJ had a lot to say leading to this game causing many to tune into this matchup. After dropping a league high 142 points week 1, OG disappointed with an abysmal 77 point performance. MTS cruised to a win but injuries to Barkley and St. Brown will have them worried going into next week.
