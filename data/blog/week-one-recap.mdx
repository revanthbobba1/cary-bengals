## Cookin’ It Up (1-0) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (0-1)
### Final Score: Cookin' It Up (115.06) - Disney Pixar Baddies (103.44)
The Baddies were able to put themselves in a position to beat the defending champs going into Monday night but found themselves disappointed as they couldn’t get the production needed from Josh Allen. CIU escaped with a 13 point win. 

## Team ETERNAL PAIN (1-0) vs Chubbin N’ Grubbin (0-1)
### Final Score: Team ETERNAL PAIN (113.48) - Chubbin N’ Grubbin (100.46)
EP were able to jump to a lead to start Sunday’s games and stayed comfortable as CNG could only produce 6.7 points combined from their WR1 and WR2. Team EP will be happy with their 13 point win as they hope to look for a switch in culture for their organization. 

## Team Drako (0-1) vs Drake and Travis (1-0)
### Final Score: Team Drako (58.78) - Drake and Travis (77.44)
Our lowest scoring matchup this week as both teams combined for 136.18 which is lower than our highest scorer of the week. Team Drako, scoring a total of 58.78 points, will have to be worried moving forward and hope Kupp will available by week 5. Drake and Travis are not in the best spot as well after losing Dobbins for the season, but they’ll be getting Kelce back and 1-0 is better than 0-1. 

## Great Waller China (0-1) vs Olave Garden (1-0)
### Final Score: Great Waller China (102.52) - Olave Garden (142.44)
Projected the lowest score of the week and being 20 point underdogs may have provided a spark to Olave Garden as they shocked the world and scored a league high 142.44 points. Their trajectory is only going up and they look like the team to beat.

## Code Monkey (1-0) vs Meet the Stevensons (0-1)
### Final Score: Code Monkey (126.34) - Meet the Stevensons (85.46)
Our primetime game came to an underwhelming conclusion as Code Monkey once again blew out their league rival opening week. Last year’s runner ups 
will have work to do if they want to be a contender.

## YoungBoys Lover (0-1) vs Los Angeles Akers (1-0)
### Final Score: YoungBoys Lover (122.84) - Los Angeles Akers (135.46)
You can call the LA Akers the Boston Globe as they bring down the YoungBoys Lover opening week. The Akers were able to take advantage of Lover’s disappointing TNF and put up the second highest score of the week at 135.46. A lot of encouraging signs for the LA Akers as they hope to take a step forward from last season.
