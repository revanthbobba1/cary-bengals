## TNF Opening Night
### Trisomy 21 at Code Monkey (PR #414)
#### CM -0.69

We kickoff the 2025 season in Lambeau Field for the annual Week 1 matchup between Trisomy 21 and Code Monkey.

After winning the championship two years ago, TJ has had a down year last season losing in the first round of the playoffs. He’ll hope his team can start the season strong and finally beat his rival Week 1.

Code Monkey on the other hand has had their best season in years. Barely sneaking into the playoffs and not even showing up to the First Round is such a great improvement for the Monkeys and it shows how poor their standards are every season. Ankith is hoping to continue his Week 1 win streak against TJ and have a reply to every thing he says until Week 14.

## Friday Night in Brazil
### Bill 👍 vs It Hurts a Little
#### IHAL -0.74

The two og teams travel to Sau Paulo to face each other in this year’s Brazil Game.

After a great season but a disappointing end for Bill 👍, he’ll look to start off his season on a high note against his Cary Rival.

The commish had yet another disappointing season but he swears he’s coming different this year. After a championships in his Fantasy Basketball and Soccer leagues, he’ll look to carry that momentum into the virtual gridiron and start the season off with a win.

We’ll see fireworks early as both teams hold players for the Eagles vs Cowboys game tonight.

## 1:00
### Stevenson and White Guys for at Bark for Daddy
#### SAWGF +2.49

This matchup in New York may get heated quick as Alvin mocked his Week 1 opponent (team name) right after the draft.

The 3x Champ had a little bit of a rebirth last season with making the final four but the team he’s drafted this year looks very dissapointing. A lot of criticism from leaguemates was shown in this week’s podcast but they’ll hope to prove everyone wrong with a win Week 1.

Bark for Daddy had a weird draft night as he was on a date during the draft. Even with the adversity he had to face, Kirk drafted a decent team on paper and he’ll look to start the season 1-0.

## 4:25
### Kamara vs the World at Jayden Jefferson Jr’s Mom
#### JJJM -5.22

The defending champs travel to Chapel Hill to take on Cotbib’s JJJM.

After a stretch of disappointing seasons, KvTW finishes on top in 2024 and all of America will see if they have the tools to repeat as champion.

JJJM will have their biggest game in years as their new head coach Bill Belichick returns back to football but this time on the virtual gridiron. The game will be sold out and the tickets will be hard to find. Because of that, JJJM comes in as the slight favorite.


## SNF
### Heterophobes Reloaded at Tet Offensive
#### HR -3.62

Lovers off the field but enemies on it, HPRL travels to Chapel Hill to take on newly relocated Tet Offensive at Hooker Fields (Carter booked Kenan).

HPRL saw themselves one play away from the playoffs and maybe even a championship but it was taken away by his rival due to an unforeseen play in the Bengals Cowboys game. Joseph has reportedly filed for divorce and hasn’t spoke to Sparsh since that terrible day.

A house separated but now together for a day in Chapel Hill, Sparsh looks for revenge on his league mates as he fell short in the final once again. The two time champ and four time finalist looks to continue his dominance and have the upper hand on his rival until Week 14.

## MNF
### Nangali’s Naners at Ladd’s Lads
#### LL -1.11

The Naners travel to Seattle to take on Ladd’s Lads at Lumen Field.

It seems like the last place Naners are locked in and were seeded at #2 in this year’s preseason poll. They’ll look to start strong and get an important win on the road.

We always expected the Lads from Ladd to make the jump into a championship team but after almost finishing last in 2024 and drafting yet another disappointing team, we may have to wait. The Lads will look to prove everyone wrong by making a statement on Monday.
