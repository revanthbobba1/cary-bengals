## TNF (Prime)
### Ladd’s Lads at Chasing an Identity 
#### Line: LADD-6.6

The Lads take the long flight to Cary to face the defending champs Chasing an Identity in a highly sought after matchup.

The Lads had an unremarkable start to their season due to three players in their starting lineup scoring less than four points. Due to their disappointment, team manager Keshav has decided to spice things up and give Ladd McConkey his pro debut.

Chasing an Identity fell short last week against their league rival. During the press conference after the game, team manger TJ said he was more upset about the Carolina Panthers and he has confidence his team will bounce back. They’ll hope their star RB Travis Etienne doesn’t fumble his starting job just like he fumbled his team’s game away. 

With the recent success both of these teams had in previous years, it is insane that one of these two teams will start 2024 with an 0-2 record. The Lads are favored to win according to ESPN BET. 

## 1:00 (CBS)
### Christian Crusaders at Code Monkey (Master) 
#### Line: CM -13.1

The Crusaders travel to DC to take on Code Monkey in this somewhat measly matchup.

After getting their first win in almost a year, Code Monkey goes into Week 2 with high hopes and great confidence to start 2-0. During Code Monkey’s press conference, team manager Ankith went on and on about how great his code is causing many reporters to fall asleep. Code Monkey will rely on their elite RB group once again to beat the Crusaders.

The Crusaders were unlucky to lose last week as a late injury report caused his Commander (CMC) to miss out on his match. They may be missing their star man again this week and hope either Joe Mixon or Chris Olave can make up for their loss. 

Code Monkey are considered the huge home favorites, but many hope they get stricken back down to the league’s jester this week. 

## 1:00 (FOX)
### Scary Terry’s Turbulators at Team Drako 
#### Line: TD -7.9

The Turbulators travel to Kenan Memorial Stadium to take on Team Drako in this highly anticipated match. 

The Turbulators are coming off a win Week 1, but many doubt their ability to contend this season as they were ranked #7 in this Week’s Manager Poll. They’ll have a great chance to prove themselves against a strong Team Drako team Sunday afternoon. 

After a strong season last year, Team Drako carried their momentum into Week 1 with a huge win against Ladd’s Lads. Many consider TD a strong contender this year and they’ll be a huge favorite to improve to 2-0.

## 4:25 (CBS)
### London is Red at My Puka Bear 
#### Line: LOND -14.1

London is Red travel to San Francisco to take on My Puka Bear.

Coming off the league’s lowest scoring performance last week, London is Red’s manager Rishi publicly shared his disappointment with his team but since showed little panic to his team’s slow start. The injury of CMC and the emergence of Jordan Mason will up their confidence going into Levi’s Stadium. London is Red will be more motivated than ever because they haven’t beat My Puka Bear in six years.

My Puka Bear looked like their vintage selves last week with a high scoring win against JJ Phoenix Rising. After being ranked #2 in this week’s poll, many would think they’d be the huge favorites this week. But losing Puka Nacua to IR and Jayden Reed losing his QB for a few weeks, their projections do not look good against London is Red. Knowing their history against Commish’s Team, they’ll be confident the team will get the job done. 

London is Red are 14 point favorites.

## SNF (NBC)
### JJ Phoenix Rising at I Cook Brown Rice 
#### Line: ICBR -3.4

JJ Phoenix Rising travel to Cary to take on I Cook Brown Rice in this week’s primetime game.

JJPR lost last week to My Puka Bear in a shootout where they allowed 143 points. They’ll hope to play better defense this week to avoid a dreaded 0-2 start.

I Cook Brown Rice was trailing most of the game last week, but thanks to Kyle Shanahan and Jake Moody, ICBR escaped with a win. They’ll hope to get more out of star WR Amon Ra St. Brown and continue to rise up the polls. 

ICBR are slight favorites due to home field advantage.

## MNF (ESPN/ABC)
### Team Super Zay’in at Kupp Kupp and Away! 
#### Line: TSZ -2

Team Super Zay’in travels to Cary to take on Kupp Kupp and Away! for this week’s MNF. 

After a statement performance last week, KKAA looks like they are the team to beat this season. Due to some questionable activity such as Puka getting hurt and Jalen Hurts forgetting how to tush push, Sparsh’s team were the highest scoring team and ranked #1 in this week’s poll. 

After being called one of the highest ceiling teams after the draft, TSZ showed their floor Week 1 in an abysmal display of Fantasy Football. Even after suffering such a terrible loss, Amogh (manager) blamed media bias for his #11 rank for this week’s poll. They’ll hope to get more out of star rookie Maserati Marv in order to take down a giant in Kupp Kupp and Away!

TSZ are slight favorites this week.
