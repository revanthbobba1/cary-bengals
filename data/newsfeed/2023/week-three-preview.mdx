## TNF (Prime Video)
### Team ETERNAL PAIN (1-1) vs YoungBoys Lover (1-1)
### Line: YBL -15.4
A huge game for both teams as they’re both 1-1 to start the season. Team EP is coming off a heartbreaking loss last week, but team manager Amogh has confidence his men will bounce back. YBL has the highest projected score of the week at 132.2 and they’ll hope the ESPN projections are correct. There aren’t too many injury concerns for both these teams this week making this an entertaining matchup.

## 1:00 (CBS)
### Code Monkey (1-1) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (1-1)
#### Line: DPB -1.9
Code Monkey’s depleted team could not get the job done last week. They’ll hope star RB A. Jones comes back as they’ll need help against a hot team of Baddies. The Baddies want to tell the league they’re looking for something long term by picking an important win week 3. 

## 1:00 (FOX)
### Team Drako (0-2) vs Cookin’ It Up (2-0)
#### Line: CIT -7.4 
Team Drako, now 0-2, wants to shock everyone by picking up their first win against first place team CIT. The defending champs haven’t looked great to start the season, but they’ve gotten the job done. Team Drako manager Carter isn’t the most confident as of now, but a late surge of points in week 2’s matchup is giving hope going into week 3.

## 4:25 (CBS)
### Meet the Stevensons (1-1)  vs Los Angeles Akers (1-1)
#### Line: Akers -4.9
Everytime these two teams face off, we know we’re in for a treat. In the last years’ regular season matchup, MTS beat the Akers by 0.1 points. In the semifinals, MTS vs LA Akers was decided on Monday Night with MTS once again escaping with a win. LA Akers will channel their inner Batman and look for vengeance in this all entertaining matchup. 

## SNF (NBC)
### Olave Garden (1-1)  vs Drake and Travis (2-0)
#### Line: D&T -13.5
This entertaining rivalry featuring former roommates always has us tuned in. Both teams have been successful these past years. I know it’s early in the season, but this game is a must win as it’ll set the tone for both teams moving forward.

## MNF (ESPN)
### Great Waller China (1-1) vs Chubbin’ N Grubbin (0-2)
#### Line: CNG -0.9
GWC coming off a huge win against the Akers week 2 will hope the momentum carries against the struggling CNG. After losing their best player for the season, CNG made the first trade of the season acquiring Raheem Mostert. They need to him to fill the void if they want to remain relevant moving forward
