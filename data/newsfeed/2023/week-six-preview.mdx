## TNF (Prime Video)
### Hammy Heroes (4-1) vs Los Angeles Akers (2-3)
#### Line: HH -4.1
The defending champ suffered their first loss of the season last week after getting steamrolled by Olave Garden. They’ll have to face off against the LA Akers this week. After a strong start to the season, the Akers have dropped back to back games and put up a sub 100 point performance against their league rival. Both teams want a bounce back victory, but the pressure will certainly be on the Commish’s team as a loss here will put them close to the bottom. 

## 1:00 (FOX)
### Team Drako (1-4) vs Olave Garden (3-2)
#### Line: Drako -3.4

Both teams coming off their highest points of the season last week as Team Drako got their first win and OG put up the league’s highest point total this season. Team Drako will look to ride their momentum by beating a strong OG team. OG wants to prove they belong at the top by repeating their performance from last week. Projecting as one of the highest scoring matchups of the week, Team Drako is shockingly favored coming into this week. 

## 1:00 (CBS) 
### JJ Waiting Room (1-4) vs Disney Pixar Baddies (2-3)
#### Line: JWR -1.6

After a shocking performance last week, JWR made a trade for this year’s #1 pick Justin Jefferson, but will be without him for four weeks due to injury. Many are questioning their decision as they need wins right now. On the other hand, the Baddies are coming off their annual win against their league rival LA Akers, and will look to improve as important players are coming off their bye week. This matchup is a must win for both teams as they’re both under .500, but JWR cannot fall into a deeper hole. 


## 4:25 (FOX)
### Certified Boys Lover (2-3) vs Drake and Travis (5-0) 
#### Line: D&L -17.1

Make that 5 for 5 for Drake and Travis as they are now the only undefeated team left. They will be the huge favorite against a struggling CBL. The Heterophobe traded away their best player JJettas because of his recent injury. Looking to pickup some wins now, they’ll have a tough task beating the red hot Drake and London. 

## SNF (NBC)
### Code Monkey (2-3) vs Team ETERNAL PAIN (3-2)
#### Line: Team EP -0.7

This week’s primetime matchup features last year’s bottom two teams. Both managers are sure last year was a fluke but Code Monkey has been struggling. On the other hand Team EP has found their stride, even though they lost last week, by being above .500 at this point. Code Monkey, coming off back to back losses, will have their #1 pick Austin Ekeler coming back as they’ll need a win this week. 

## MNF (ESPN)
### Great Waller China (2-3) vs AmOn Another Level (3-2)
#### Line: AmOn -1.2

After losing week 3 to the Commish, AmOn’s manager TJ had a lot to say to and about the league in the press conference. His words seemed to work as his team are coming off back to back wins. His opponent, the 3x champ, got a must needed win against their league rival last week. GWC is looking for a way to get back to the top but AmOn will stand in their way. This is one for the closest projected matchups of the week.
